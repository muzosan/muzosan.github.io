Neler silinir, neler saklanır?

Silinir: hesap kimliği, giriş bilgisi, kullanıcı adı, tuttuğunuz takım, dereceli puan, rütbe ve sezon rozetleri. Maç odaları zaten 24 saat sonra otomatik silinir.

Saklanabilir: uygulama içi satın alma kayıtları, yasal yükümlülükler nedeniyle Google Play tarafından saklanır. Telefonunuzdaki dil tercihi ve rekorlar uygulamayı kaldırdığınızda silinir.

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Volea.
  2. Tap the profile icon at the top right.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Your account, username, profile, ranked rating and rank are permanently deleted.

By email

If you cannot access the app, email muazonurluer2312@gmail.com with the subject "Volea account deletion" and include your username. Your request will be fulfilled within 30 days and you will be notified.

What is deleted and what is kept?

Deleted: account ID, sign-in record, username, favourite team, ranked rating, rank and season badges. Match rooms are already deleted automatically after 24 hours.

Kept: in-app purchase records are retained by Google Play for legal reasons. Language preference and records on your phone are removed when you uninstall the app.
